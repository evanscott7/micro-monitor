Agent shapes

Each agent keeps one shape for life, so you learn them like faces. The fill tells you the state. No checkmarks: a solid white shape means "finished, not looked at yet".

States

Outline = idle. Dot-filled = working. Solid white = just finished. Terracotta with a glow = needs you. The same dots appear on anything you hover, so dots always mean "someone is working here".

Day arc

Borrowed from the "diffused light" gauge. Terracotta is daylight, lavender is evening before bed. The thicker black overlay with a dashed white rim is time already spent, so the arc burns down through the day. The dark dot marks sunset.

Principles

Calm technology for an ADHD brain.

One thing at a time

The home screen shows the single most important item. Anything else waits behind a click.

Still unless something changed

Nothing loops or pulses. Motion is spent only when the top item changes, so motion always means something.

One loud color

Terracotta means "an agent needs you". Green means finished, deep violet means a calendar event.

Time as space

The day arc burns down as the day goes. You feel the evening shrinking instead of reading a clock.

Capture goes away

Thoughts go to an inbox you never see here. Empty your head without adding a list to stare at.

Dots mean work

A working agent fills with dots. Hover anything and it fills with the same dots, swelling around your pointer. Where an agent and you are both on something, the dots overlap.

How the prototype becomes the real screen. Nothing here is installed yet.

Agents · Claude Code hooks

One async hook appends a line per event. Grok Bot writes the same shape.

jq -c '{t:now, src:"claude",
  ev:.hook_event_name, sid:.session_id,
  cwd:.cwd, n:(.notification_type//null)}' \
  >> ~/.lighthouse/events.ndjson
  1. UserPromptSubmit → working
  2. Notification (permission_prompt) → needs you
  3. Stop → just finished
  4. SessionEnd → shape removed
